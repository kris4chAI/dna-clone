# Niche Consulting brand guardian (audit mode)

Use this skill on slide titles, subtitles and body text before the red team (stage 8) and before anything is sent to the client.

## Rules checked

| Rule | Check | Severity |
|---|---|---|
| Titles | Three words or fewer; the message goes in the subtitle | Amber |
| Case | Sentence case titles (not Title Case, not ALL CAPS) | Amber |
| Honest labels | Money, percentages, TCO, ROI and savings carry INDICATIVE; designs and mock-ups carry ILLUSTRATIVE; contract, security, RACI and risks carry FOR JOINT REVIEW | Red |
| Competitors | No competitor or rival-platform names in client-facing content | Red |
| Logos | Every partner, client or vendor logo is approved or flagged | Amber |
| Voice | Plain language: no filler such as "leverage", "synergy", "world-class", "cutting-edge", "best-in-class" | Green |
| Consistency | The same figure (estate size, dates, counts, prices) everywhere | Red |

## Output

A table with Rule · Where · Issue · Fix · Severity, then fix what can be fixed without inventing facts.

The firm's full brand book (palette, type, logo, iconography) stays in the original brand-guardian package; this audit covers the rules the app can check automatically.
